FAADHKURUNI ADHKURKUM - laser files
Board: 680 x 324 mm visible.  Material: 3 x 12in (304.8 mm) sheets of 3 mm plywood.

COLOURS (one layer each in LightBurn / Creality software)
  BLACK = cut through - all pieces. Turn on "cut inner shapes first".
  BLUE  = cut through, LAST - the stencil outline (frees the leftover sheet).
  RED   = light score only - labels on the leftover/stencil, never on a piece.
Files are true size in mm (SVG and DXF are identical).

SHEETS
  sheet1_background_top_left        89 pieces, cut in place: the leftover sheet is the stencil.
  sheet2_background_bottom_right    45 pieces, cut in place; its stencil edge hugs the sheet-1 letters.
  sheet3_verse_and_edge_pieces      26 verse pieces in place (leftover = verse stencil)
                                    + 9 numbered background edge pieces.
  Hidden spacers (20) are part of sheets 1/2: they sit under the verse so every verse piece is supported.

ORDER
  1. Paint the board. Stencil 1: top edge on the board's top edge, left edge 204 mm from the left edge.
     Glue each piece into its own hole, lift the stencil.
  2. Stencil 2: bottom edge on the board's bottom edge (left edge 348 mm from the left);
     it locks against the sheet-1 letters. Glue, lift.
  3. Edge pieces 1-9: see assembly_map.png.
  4. Rug pieces (trim the frayed edge to meet the letters).
  5. Verse stencil on top of the brown layer: top-left corner 345 mm from the left, 46 mm from the top.
     Glue the verse pieces (paint silver first).

Checks run on the exact cut lines: no piece merges, nothing crosses the board or rug edge,
smallest gap between pieces 1.2 mm, verse supported >= 40% of its area.
